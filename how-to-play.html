How it works

There are two websites.SoleX is the shop where you buy. SoleSmith is your game, where your shoes live and you sell, trade and earn.

Make an account

On SoleX or SoleSmith: press Sign in

Make one account. Use the same email and password on both websites.You start with 1,000 coins.

Buy shoes

Go to SoleX

Open SoleX. Click a shoe, pick a size, type how many, and press Buy now.Your shoes go to your inventory in SoleSmith.

Which shoe?

In SoleX: use the filter buttons

Low shoes are normal. High shoes are high-tops. Premium shoes cost the most.Premium prices move the most every 3 minutes.

Prices move

In SoleX: open any shoe to see its chart

Prices change every 3 minutes. Buy low, sell high!

Drops

At the top of SoleX

A drop comes every 10 minutes. Only 200 coins! First to click wins.You get a beep 1 minute before.

Your shoes

Go to SoleSmith: My inventory

Every shoe you buy shows up in My inventory.From there you can sell it, list it, or show it off in your showcase.

Sell to offers

In SoleSmith: My inventory, then Get offers

Press Get offers on a shoe. Buyers make offers. Pick one and it sells.Offers last 3 minutes.

Sell in bulk

In SoleSmith: My inventory, then Sell in bulk

Got a big stack? Pick the shoe, type how many (or press All), and get offers.Every pair sells at the same price. Grail shoes cannot be sold to offers.

The fee

In SoleSmith: Government tab shows today's fee

When you sell to offers or to players, a small fee is taken from the sale.You always see how much you will get before you accept.

Sell to players

In SoleSmith: My inventory, then List for players

Pick your own price. Other players buy it in the Player market tab.You can cancel your listing any time.

Ask a player

In SoleSmith: click a player name to open their profile

Want a shoe someone owns? Open their profile and press Request.They get a message in their Inbox, even if they are away. They can accept or decline.

Collections

In SoleSmith: Collections tab

Collect all 10 shoes in a set for a big prize.The grail only comes from a drop, and only ONE exists in the whole game!

Workers

In SoleSmith: Workers tab

Hire up to 5 workers. They pay you coins every 2 minutes.

Missions and ranking

In SoleSmith: Missions and Leaderboard tabs

Do daily missions for coins. Climb the leaderboard.

Giveaways

In SoleSmith: Giveaways tab

The owner lists shoes in Giveaways. The first players to press the button win!

Good to know

A few buttons that help.Also check the Hall of fame tab to see who owns each grail.
