SoleSmith & SoleX

How to play

Collect sneakers, trade with other players, and get rich. Here is everything in simple words.

1. How to start

  • There are two websites. SoleX is the shop where you buy shoes. SoleSmith is your game, where your shoes live.
  • Press Sign in, type an email and a password, and press Create account. Use the same email and password on both websites.
  • You start with 1,000 coins. Coins are the money in the game.
Your shoes are in My inventory in SoleSmith. Press Change name to pick a name for the leaderboard.

2. Buying from the shop (SoleX)

  • Click a shoe, pick a size, type how many pairs you want (1 to 10), and press Buy now. The shoes go straight into your inventory.
  • Prices change every 3 minutes by a few coins, up or down. The chart shows how the price moved. Buy low, sell high.
  • Shoes called Low are normal sneakers. Shoes called High are high-tops and cost a little more.
  • Use the filter buttons on top to find Low, High, a rarity, or one of the collections.

3. Drops

  • A new drop appears at the top of SoleX every 10 minutes. A drop is a special shoe that is not in the shop.
  • Every drop costs only 200 coins, but there are not many. The first person to click gets it.
  • You cannot pick the size. The size is chosen for you when the drop appears.
  • There are two kinds of drops: cheap drops (worth about 1,000 to 3,000 coins, 5 to 10 available) and good drops (worth about 10,000 to 30,000 coins, only 1 or 2 available).
  • Sometimes the drop is a GRAIL. See the Collections part below.
A shoe you get for 200 coins can be worth thousands. Stay on SoleX and be fast!

4. Selling your shoes

Open My inventory in SoleSmith. Every shoe has two ways to sell:

  • Get offers: you get 3 random offers from buyers. Pick one and the shoe is sold right away. Offers last 3 minutes. A normal shoe only makes a few coins of profit. A drop shoe sells for around what it is worth, so the profit is big.
  • List for players: you pick your own price and other players can buy it on the Player market. You get the coins when someone buys it. You can cancel your listing at any time.
Grail shoes cannot be sold to offers. You can only sell them to players, or keep them.

5. Collections and grails

A collection is a set of 10 shoes with the same first name. Nine of them are sold in the SoleX shop. The tenth is the GRAIL.

  • A grail never appears in the shop. It only comes from a drop, and you have to wait for it.
  • There is only one copy of each grail in the whole game. When someone buys it, it will never drop again.
  • Own all 10 shoes of a set (in your inventory) and press Claim in the Collections tab to get the prize. You keep your shoes after you claim it, and you can claim each set once.

7. Daily missions

Open the Missions tab. Finish a mission and press Claim to get coins. Missions start over every day.

  • Buy 3 shoes from SoleX: 150 coins
  • Sell 2 shoes to offers: 100 coins
  • Grab a shoe from a drop: 300 coins
  • List a shoe for other players: 100 coins
  • Buy a shoe from another player: 150 coins
  • Sell a shoe to another player: 200 coins

8. Net worth and the leaderboard

  • Net worth is your coins plus the value of every shoe you own. You see it at the top of SoleSmith.
  • The Leaderboard shows the 20 richest players. Climb it by buying smart, selling well, and collecting rare shoes.
Quick plan: buy a cheap shoe, hire your first worker, check every drop, and finish your daily missions.

9. Profiles, requests and showcase

  • Click any player name (on the leaderboard, the player market or the hall of fame) to open their profile. You see their net worth, their finished collections, their showcase and their shoes.
  • Want a shoe from someone's inventory? Press Request this shoe and type how many coins you offer. They get a message in their Inbox. If they are online they see it right away. If they are away, it waits for them until they come back.
  • In the Inbox they press Accept (the shoe goes to you and the coins go to them) or Decline. You get a message back about what they chose. You can cancel a request you sent while it is waiting.
  • The Showcase is up to 3 of your best shoes shown at the top of your profile. In My inventory, press Add to showcase on a shoe.
Everyone who plays can see your profile and your shoes. A request is only an offer. Nothing happens unless you accept it.

10. Grail hall of fame

The Hall of fame tab lists every grail shoe in the game. For each one it shows if it is still hiding in the drops, or who owns it and since when. Click the owner's name to see their profile.

11. Dark and light mode

Press the Light mode / Dark mode button at the top of the page to change how the websites look. The choice is remembered for next time.
